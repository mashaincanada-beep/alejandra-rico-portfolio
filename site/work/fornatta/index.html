Fornatta Pizza

A warm Italian identity for a wood fired pizzeria in Niagara Falls.

Discipline
Visual identity
Client
Fornatta
Year
2026
Role
Brand identity, menu, packaging
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Brief

Fornatta brings an authentic Italian pizza experience to Niagara Falls, a different option from the American style pizza common in the area. The brand centers on quality ingredients and wood fired preparation.

Approach

A classic serif wordmark, deep tomato reds and a warm cream inspired by Italian tradition. The goal is more than food: a welcoming place that connects people with the flavor and atmosphere of Italian pizza.

Result

An identity applied to the menu, pizza boxes, staff shirts, tote bags and street signage ahead of opening.
