JCA Hydraulic and Hydrologic Engineering

A precise, confident identity for an engineering firm working on water management and flood risk.

Discipline
Visual identity
Client
JCA Ingeniería Hidráulica e Hidrología
Year
2026
Role
Brand identity, applications
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Brief

JCA delivers technical solutions for water management, drainage systems, flood risk and infrastructure planning. The brand needed to communicate precision, trust and expertise.

Approach

A professional visual language with graphic elements inspired by water flow and engineering analysis. A sharp, angular monogram in signal orange pairs with deep navy and electric blue.

Result

An identity system shown on uniforms, equipment, hoardings and billboards.
