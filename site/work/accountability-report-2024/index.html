Accountability Report 2024

A newspaper that turns a councilman’s first year in office into a clear, engaging read.

Discipline
Editorial design
Client
Cali City Council
Year
2025
Role
Newspaper design and layout
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

View publication

Brief

Present the main achievements of Edison Giraldo’s first year on Cali City Council: downtown revitalization, local commerce, public space, mobility, community support and political oversight.

Approach

Turn an institutional report into an accessible product. Bold typography, photography, structured information and campaign inspired graphics communicate impact directly and memorably.

Result

Together with the 2025 edition, about 2,000 copies distributed in downtown Cali.
