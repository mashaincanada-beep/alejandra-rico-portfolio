Energizar

A subtle rebrand for a Colombian energy distribution company with more than 40 years in business.

Discipline
Rebrand
Client
Energizar
Year
2025
Role
Research, rebrand, brand applications
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Brief

Energizar provides energy distribution solutions for the commercial and construction sectors in Valle del Cauca and across Colombia. The goal was to modernize the identity while keeping the elements people already recognize: an evolution, not a reset.

Approach

I interviewed the owner one on one to understand his needs and expectations, then researched how energy and energy waves are represented visually. Concentric rings around the lowercase e carry that idea of force and expansion into the new mark.

Result

A cleaner, more modern brand that communicates better on social media and holds up on site, from hard hats and uniforms to buildings and flags.
