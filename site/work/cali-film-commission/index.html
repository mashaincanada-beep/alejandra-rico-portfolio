Cali Film Commission

A website that makes it easier to get a permit to film in Cali’s public space.

Discipline
Web and UX
Client
Secretariat of Economic Development, Cali City Hall
Year
2025
Role
Website design

Brief

Cali Film connects audiovisual producers with the opportunities, benefits and incentives for filming in the city. Getting permission to shoot in public space meant several forms and offices.

Approach

The site explains the process step by step: what to do, which forms to download, when and where to apply, exemptions, a fee calculator and a timeline of the request, in a bold, colorful system tied to the city.

Result

One place that brings filming procedures together and promotes Cali as a destination for national and international productions.
