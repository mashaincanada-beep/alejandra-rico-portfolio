Music and Film Event Design

Printed pieces for events and initiatives in Cali’s music and film sectors, including Cali Ruge.

Discipline
Print and events
Client
Secretariat of Economic Development, Cali City Hall
Year
2025
Role
Event identity, print and promotional design
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

Brief

Promote cultural activities and support local creative industries with materials for events connected to the city’s music and film sectors, including Cali Ruge, an initiative that showcases emerging artistic talent.

Approach

Give each event its own identity through clear, engaging editorial and promotional design, from street posters to packaging and collectible cards.

Result

A family of printed pieces used at events and in public space across the city.
